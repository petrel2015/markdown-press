Faces bundled (5 faces, TTF):

  Spectral-Regular.ttf        400 normal   (latin serif — narrative voice)
  Spectral-Italic.ttf         400 italic
  Spectral-Bold.ttf           700 normal
  IBMPlexMono-Regular.ttf     400 normal   (data voice)
  IBMPlexMono-Bold.ttf        700 normal

Latin glyphs only — CJK text falls back to Noto Serif SC / Songti SC /
STSong via the --serif stack in css/tokens.css. Chinese body text must
never render in a sans-serif face.
