ET Book — vendored font faces (research-report skin)
======================================================

Source ...: https://github.com/edwardtufte/tufte-css  (et-book/ directory)
License ..: SIL Open Font License 1.1 — free to embed, redistribute
            and use in commercial projects. Full text:
            https://openfontlicense.org/open-font-license-official-text/

Faces bundled (only the three this app needs, woff only):

  et-book-roman-line-figures.woff          400 normal
  et-book-display-italic-figures.woff      400 italic
    (upstream name: et-book-display-italic-old-style-figures)
  et-book-bold-line-figures.woff           700 normal

Latin glyphs only — CJK text falls back to Noto Serif SC / Songti SC /
SimSun via the --serif stack in css/tokens.css.
